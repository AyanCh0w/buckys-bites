Privacy policy.

This policy covers the Bucky’s Bites dining app and bucky.eat. Bucky’s Bites is an independent student project for people dining at Santa Clara University.

Browsing and signing in

You can browse menus and public reviews as a guest. The app caches menu data and saves choices such as onboarding completion and guest dietary filters on your device.

If you sign in with Apple, Apple supplies an account identifier and may provide your email address, including a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email. Supabase authenticates your account. The iOS app keeps your session in the device’s secure storage.

Meals, preferences, and goals

When you log food, we store the dish, portion, meal period, and eating time with your account in Supabase. We use this information to show your history, calculate nutrition totals, and determine which dishes you can review. Saved preferences include dietary filters, starred kitchens, and nutrient goals. Other diners cannot see your private food log, portions, or goals.

The optional goal estimator calculates targets on your device using the measurements and activity level you enter. Applying an estimate saves the resulting nutrient targets to your profile.

Public reviews and votes

When you post a rating or written review, other diners can see your public display name, chosen avatar, score, and any comment you include. The app can show the meal, date, and your reviewer score. Votes help rank reviews and affect that score.

The developer can access meal logs and ratings to operate the service, investigate issues, and understand usage through an internal dashboard.

The website, waitlist, and support

Vercel serves bucky.eat and processes technical information such as IP addresses and requested pages to deliver the site. The waitlist sends your name, email, and any optional note to Google Forms when you submit it. We use your response to manage launch updates and feedback. You can ask us to remove your waitlist entry.

If you contact support by email, we use your address, message, and any attachments to respond and investigate the request.

Retention and your choices

Account data supports your saved history and preferences while you use the service. When you delete a food log, a minimal record of the dish and eating time remains for review and voting eligibility. A published review can remain after its food log is removed.

To export your data or request removal of your account, meals, reviews, and preferences, open Me → Privacy while signed in. A deletion request goes to the developer for processing. You can also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, or to withdraw a waitlist signup. We may need to verify that the request belongs to you.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, email bucky@redbot.pro. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated effective date.
