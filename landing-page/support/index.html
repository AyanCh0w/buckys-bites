App support.

Spotted the wrong menu, need help signing in, or have a question about your data? Get in touch with the Bucky’s Bites team.

Contact support

Email bucky@redbot.pro. For an app issue, tell us which screen you were on and what you expected to happen. Please leave out passwords, sign-in codes, and personal health details.

Report a menu error

Include the kitchen, dish, date, and meal period so we can find the listing. If you need to confirm ingredients or allergens before ordering, ask the dining staff serving the dish.

Report a review

Send the dish name, the reviewer’s public display name, and the date or a short excerpt that identifies the review. Let us know what concerns you.

Sign in or write a review

You can browse menus and read reviews as a guest. To log a meal, open a dish and choose Add to today’s food, then sign in with Apple. A recent meal log for that dish lets you write a review.

Export or delete your data

Sign in, open Me, and tap Privacy at the bottom. Choose Export my data for a copy or Request account deletion to send a deletion request. If you can’t access the app, contact support.
